# Contributing to SysFigure Atlas

Thanks for helping systems researchers find and understand useful figure references.
English and Chinese reports are both welcome.

## Corrections

Use the [figure correction form](https://github.com/cookiesheep/sysfigure-atlas/issues/new?template=figure-correction.md).
Include the figure ID shown in the detail view, what is wrong, and the source page,
caption, or visible image element that supports your correction. Separate observations
in the figure from claims supported only by the paper text. Do not infer a speedup,
resource count, or algorithm from the drawing style alone.

## Missing references

Use the [paper suggestion form](https://github.com/cookiesheep/sysfigure-atlas/issues/new?template=paper-suggestion.md).
Provide the paper's official or author-hosted link, figure number, and what the figure
helps explain. Coverage gaps, readable examples, and distinct visual structures are
more useful than simply adding more figures from the same source. A suggestion does
not guarantee inclusion.

## Search and interface feedback

Please include the query, active filters, expected figure or pattern, and actual result.
Screenshots help, but remove personal notes and other information you do not want public.

## Pull requests and collection integration

Open an issue first for large data imports, schema changes, or integration with another
gallery so we can agree on scope and avoid duplicate work. Small, explained fixes are
welcome as PRs. This repository is an exported static release: publication changes also
need to be incorporated into the maintained source/export process so they survive the
next release. The collection/review pipeline itself is not in this repository.

Keep figure identity, source version, crop, caption, and explanation aligned. Do not
replace a scientific caption with a generated interpretation. Do not mark a proposed
correction as already reviewed. Avoid uploading full PDF collections, credentials, or
personal browser storage.

For collection reuse or a systems-focused module in another gallery, discuss the data
format, attribution, maintenance responsibilities, and applicable permissions first.
Third-party paper figures retain their original rights; we cannot grant those rights
on behalf of their authors or publishers.
